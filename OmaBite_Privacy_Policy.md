<h2 align="center">Full policy</h2>

## 1. Overview

### OmaBite is designed to keep your data under your control.

- Your food diary, meals, weight, water, and all in-app content stay on your device (and in your personal iCloud, if you enable sync).
- We do not operate our own servers for your diary data.
- We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not build profiles about you, and we do not sell data.
- The only data that ever leaves your device is the content you choose to submit to the optional Nutrition Autofill feature — either a nutrition label image, or text you enter or paste — which is sent to a third-party AI service to extract nutrition information.

## 2. Data We Process

### A. Data stored on your device and in iCloud (if enabled)

The App stores your food diary content locally on your device — including your meals, ingredients, nutrition values, water and weight records, settings, and any other content you create in the App. If you enable iCloud sync, this content is also stored in your personal iCloud account. This data is used only to provide your personal in-app experience and to sync through iCloud if you choose to enable it.

### B. Nutrition Autofill content (images or text)

If you use the optional Nutrition Autofill feature, the content you submit is sent to a third-party AI service provider for one purpose only: to recognize nutrition information and return structured data so the App can autofill nutrition fields.

The feature supports two types of input:

- **Scan Nutrition Facts** — a nutrition label photo you capture with the camera.
- **Fill from text** — text you type or paste describing the nutrition information.

This feature is optional. You must grant consent before any content is sent, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. See "How to Withdraw Your Consent for Nutrition Autofill" below.

### C. Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase Pro features or a subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information. We may receive from Apple a transaction identifier and subscription status in order to unlock and maintain access to Pro features on your device.

### D. Device permissions

The App may request the following permissions, which you may grant or deny, and later revoke in your device settings:

- **Camera** — to capture a nutrition label image for the Nutrition Autofill feature. This permission is requested by the system the first time you use the camera.

## 3. How We Use Data

### We use data only for the following purposes:

- to store and display your food diary information on your device;
- to sync your data through iCloud if you enable that feature;
- to process the image or text you voluntarily submit through the Nutrition Autofill feature;
- to return recognized nutrition data and autofill relevant fields in the App;
- to provide and unlock Pro features through the App Store.

## 4. Third-Party Processing for Nutrition Autofill

### When you use the optional Nutrition Autofill feature, the content you submit — either an image or text — is sent to a third-party AI service provider to extract nutrition information.

**Current AI provider:** We currently use OpenAI to process Nutrition Autofill images. OpenAI's handling of API submissions is governed by its own policies and terms, available at <https://openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies>.

**Changes in provider:** We may change AI providers over time, for example to improve accuracy, reliability, or privacy protections. This Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect the current provider. If a change materially affects how your data is processed, we will notify you in the App before the change takes effect.

You should avoid submitting images or text that contain unnecessary personal or sensitive information unrelated to the nutrition information.

## 5. How to Withdraw Your Consent for Nutrition Autofill

The Nutrition Autofill feature only sends an image to OpenAI after you have explicitly granted consent. You can withdraw this consent at any time: **More (on iPhone) → Legal & Privacy → Nutrition Autofill Consent → Revoke.**

### After you withdraw consent:

- No image will be sent to OpenAI from this feature.
- You can continue to use all other features of OmaBite, including manually entering nutrition values. You may grant and withdraw your consent as many times as you wish. Granting consent does not automatically send any data — content is only sent when you actively submit an image or text through the Nutrition Autofill feature.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

### On-device and iCloud data

Your diary data and app content remain stored:

- on your device; and
- in your personal iCloud account, if you enable iCloud sync.

This data remains there until you delete it, disable sync, delete the App's data, or remove it from your iCloud account according to Apple's systems and settings.

### Nutrition Autofill submissions

Images and text submitted through Nutrition Autofill may be temporarily processed by the third-party AI provider as needed to complete your request and according to that provider's own policies and retention practices. OmaBite does not store these submissions on any server.

## 7. Legal Bases for Processing

### Where required by applicable law (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR), we process personal data on the following bases:

- **Performance of a contract:** to provide the App features you request, such as storing your diary data and returning nutrition autofill results.
- **Consent:** when you choose to use optional features such as Nutrition Autofill, enable iCloud sync, or grant permissions such as camera. You may withdraw consent at any time. For Nutrition Autofill specifically, you may withdraw consent at any time via **More (on iPhone) → Legal & Privacy → Nutrition Autofill Consent → Revoke.**
- **Legitimate interests:** where applicable, to provide and improve the App in a privacy-respecting manner consistent with this Policy.

## 8. Sharing of Data

### We only share data externally in the following limited case:

- content you voluntarily submit through Nutrition Autofill — either an image or text — is sent to the third-party AI provider solely to recognize nutrition information and return structured results to the App.

## 9. Your Choices

### You can choose whether to use certain features:

- You may use the App without using Nutrition Autofill (neither image nor text input).
- You may withdraw your consent for Nutrition Autofill at any time via **More (on iPhone) → Legal & Privacy → Nutrition Autofill Consent → Revoke.**
- You may manually enter nutrition information instead of using Nutrition Autofill.
- You may choose whether to enable iCloud sync.
- You may grant or revoke camera or photo access in your device settings at any time.
- You may delete your entries and other content from within the App.

## 10. Your Rights and Data Control

Because The App doesn't operate its own backend servers, your data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account, under your direct control. You can view, edit, and delete your entries at any time from within the App. To remove all data permanently, delete the App and manage related data through your iOS iCloud settings. We have no way to access or delete your data remotely.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA — including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold your data on our servers, most of these rights are exercised directly through the App and your iCloud settings.

## 11. Children's Privacy

The App isn't intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/UK, where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 12. International Data Transfers

If the Nutrition Autofill provider processes your submission in a country different from your own, your image or text may be transferred internationally as part of providing the result. Any such transfer is limited to what is necessary to perform the request you made and is subject to the provider's own safeguards and policies.

## 13. Third-Party Services

### The App may rely on third-party services that are independent from us, including:

- Apple iCloud, if you enable iCloud sync;
- Apple's App Store, for purchases and subscriptions;
- OpenAI, for Nutrition Autofill (image and text processing).
- Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy terms and policies.

## 14. Security

We take reasonable steps to minimize data processing and to design the App so that your data stays under your control on your device and in iCloud if enabled. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the 'Last updated' date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App.
