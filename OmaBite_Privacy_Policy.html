Privacy Policy of OmaBite

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Kehan Chen ("we," "us," or "our") operates OmaBite (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App processes, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Contact Information

The Developer: Kehan Chen

Email: support@omabite.com

Full policy

1. Overview

OmaBite is designed to keep your data under your control.

  • Your food diary, meals, weight, water, and other in-app content you create stay on your device (and in your personal iCloud, if you enable sync).
  • We do not operate our own servers for your diary data.
  • Limited data may leave your device in two cases: when you actively use the optional Nutrition Autofill feature, and when the App sends limited subscription paywall and purchase-flow diagnostic information to help troubleshoot and improve the subscription experience.

2. Data We Process

A. Data stored on your device and in iCloud (if enabled)

The App stores your food diary content locally on your device — including your meals, ingredients, nutrition values, water and weight records, settings, and any other content you create in the App. If you enable iCloud sync, this content is also stored in your personal iCloud account. This data is used only to provide your personal in-app experience and to sync through iCloud if you choose to enable it.

B. Nutrition Autofill content (images or text)

If you use the optional Nutrition Autofill feature, the content you submit is sent to a third-party service provider for one purpose only: to recognize nutrition information and return structured data so the App can autofill nutrition fields.

The feature supports two types of input:

  • Scan Nutrition Facts — a nutrition label photo you capture with the camera.
  • Fill from text — text you type or paste describing the nutrition information.

This feature is optional. You must grant consent before any content is sent, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. See "How to Withdraw Your Consent for Nutrition Autofill" below.

C. Subscriptions

If you subscribe to OmaBite, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information. We may receive from Apple a transaction identifier and subscription status in order to verify your subscription and provide access to the App on your device.

D. Subscription paywall and purchase-flow diagnostics

The App may collect limited diagnostic information about the subscription paywall and purchase flow. This may include paywall interaction events, selected subscription option, purchase-flow result, App Store storefront country or region, app version, and event time.

We use this information to troubleshoot subscription issues, understand whether the paywall and purchase flow work correctly, and improve the subscription experience.

E. Device permissions

The App may request the following permissions, which you may grant or deny, and later revoke in your device settings:

  • Camera — to capture a nutrition label image for the Nutrition Autofill feature. This permission is requested by the system the first time you use the camera.

3. How We Use Data

We use data only for the following purposes:

  • to store and display your food diary information on your device;
  • to sync your data through iCloud if you enable that feature;
  • to process the image or text you voluntarily submit through the Nutrition Autofill feature;
  • to return recognized nutrition data and autofill relevant fields in the App;
  • to verify your subscription status and provide access to the App through the App Store;
  • to troubleshoot subscription paywall and purchase-flow issues and improve the subscription experience.

4. Third-Party Processing for Nutrition Autofill

When you use the optional Nutrition Autofill feature, the content you submit — either an image or text — is sent to a third-party service provider to extract nutrition information.

Current service providers: We currently use OpenAI to process Nutrition Autofill images and text for most users. For users in Mainland China, Hong Kong, and Macao, we may use Alibaba Cloud's Qwen API to process Nutrition Autofill images and text.

OpenAI's handling of API submissions is governed by its own policies and terms: https://openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies

For users whose Nutrition Autofill submissions are processed through Alibaba Cloud's Qwen API, Alibaba Cloud's handling of submitted content is governed by its own policies and terms, including Alibaba Cloud Model Studio's security and privacy documentation, Alibaba Cloud's privacy policy, and the Alibaba Cloud Model Studio Service Agreement. More details are provided in our Nutrition Autofill Privacy Notice: https://12ihifn88a.github.io/OmaBite/OmaBite_Autofill_Privacy_Notice.html

Changes in provider: We may change service providers over time, for example to improve accuracy, reliability, or privacy protections. This Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect the current providers. If a change materially affects how your data is processed, we will notify you in the App before the change takes effect.

You should avoid submitting images or text that contain unnecessary personal or sensitive information unrelated to the nutrition information.

5. How to Withdraw Your Consent for Nutrition Autofill

The Nutrition Autofill feature only sends images or text to the applicable Nutrition Autofill service provider after you have explicitly granted consent. You can withdraw this consent at any time: More (on iPhone) → Legal & Privacy → Nutrition Autofill Consent → Revoke.

After you withdraw consent:

  • No image or text will be sent to any Nutrition Autofill service provider from this feature.
  • You can continue to use all other features of OmaBite, including manually entering nutrition values. You may grant and withdraw your consent as many times as you wish. Granting consent does not automatically send any data — content is only sent when you actively submit an image or text through the Nutrition Autofill feature.

6. Data Storage and Retention

On-device and iCloud data

Your diary data and app content remain stored:

  • on your device; and
  • in your personal iCloud account, if you enable iCloud sync.

This data remains there until you delete it, disable sync, delete the App's data, or remove it from your iCloud account according to Apple's systems and settings.

Nutrition Autofill submissions

Images and text submitted through Nutrition Autofill may be temporarily processed by the applicable third-party service provider as needed to complete your request and according to that provider's own policies and retention practices. OmaBite does not store these submissions on any server.

Subscription paywall and purchase-flow diagnostics

Subscription paywall and purchase-flow diagnostic information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to troubleshoot subscription issues, review paywall performance, and improve the subscription experience. We may delete or aggregate this information when it is no longer needed.

Because this diagnostic information is designed not to directly identify you, we may not be able to locate or delete a specific user's individual diagnostic events.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Where required by applicable law (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR), we process personal data on the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the App features you request, such as storing your diary data and returning Nutrition Autofill results.
  • Consent: when you choose to use optional features such as Nutrition Autofill, enable iCloud sync, or grant permissions such as camera access. You may withdraw consent at any time. For Nutrition Autofill specifically, you may withdraw consent at any time via More (on iPhone) → Legal & Privacy → Nutrition Autofill Consent → Revoke.
  • Legitimate interests: where applicable, to provide, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the App in a privacy-respecting manner consistent with this Policy, including limited subscription paywall and purchase-flow diagnostics.

8. Sharing of Data

We only share data externally in the following limited cases:

  • Content you voluntarily submit through Nutrition Autofill — either an image or text — is sent to the third-party service provider solely to recognize nutrition information and return structured results to the App.
  • Limited subscription paywall and purchase-flow diagnostic information may be transmitted to infrastructure we use to store these diagnostic events, solely to troubleshoot subscription issues and improve the subscription experience.

9. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use certain features:

  • You may use the App without using Nutrition Autofill (neither image nor text input).
  • You may withdraw your consent for Nutrition Autofill at any time via More (on iPhone) → Legal & Privacy → Nutrition Autofill Consent → Revoke.
  • You may manually enter nutrition information instead of using Nutrition Autofill.
  • You may choose whether to enable iCloud sync.
  • You may grant or revoke camera access in your device settings at any time.
  • You may delete your entries and other content from within the App.

10. Your Rights and Data Control

Your main app content, such as your food diary, meals, ingredients, recipes, weight, water records, and settings, stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account if you enable iCloud sync. You can view, edit, and delete this content from within the App. To remove this app content permanently, delete the App and manage related data through your iOS iCloud settings.

Limited data that leaves your device, such as Nutrition Autofill submissions and subscription paywall and purchase-flow diagnostic information, is handled as described in the relevant sections of this Policy. Because some diagnostic information is designed not to directly identify you, we may not be able to locate or delete a specific user's individual diagnostic events.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA — including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we do not operate our own backend servers for your main app content, most rights related to that content are exercised directly through the App and your iCloud settings. For limited data that leaves your device, such as Nutrition Autofill submissions or diagnostic information, you may contact us at support@omabite.com.

11. Children's Privacy

The App isn't intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/UK, where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. International Data Transfers

If a third-party service provider or infrastructure provider processes Nutrition Autofill submissions or subscription paywall and purchase-flow diagnostic information in a country or region different from your own, that data may be transferred internationally or across regions as part of providing the relevant feature or diagnostic function. Any such transfer is limited to what is necessary for the purposes described in this Policy and is subject to the provider's own safeguards and policies.

13. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party services that are independent from us, including:

  • Apple iCloud, if you enable iCloud sync;
  • Apple’s App Store, for subscriptions and subscription status verification;
  • OpenAI, for Nutrition Autofill image and text processing for most users;
  • Alibaba Cloud Qwen API, for Nutrition Autofill image and text processing for users in Mainland China, Hong Kong, and Macao;
  • Cloudflare, for limited subscription paywall and purchase-flow diagnostics.

Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy terms and policies.

14. Security

We take reasonable steps to minimize data processing and to design the App so that your main app content stays under your control on your device and in iCloud if enabled. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App.
